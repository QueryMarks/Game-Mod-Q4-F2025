This file explains the CARD BATTLER mod for Quake 4. To play this mod, place the coolmod folder in your Quake 4 directory. Select it from the mods list after starting the game.

You can play this mod right from the main menu! Click CARD BATTLE to fight the opponent, DECK EDIT to edit your deck, and SHOP to shop. Your credits are displayed in the main menu and in the shop.



/// BATTLE ///

This is the main event! Click CARD BATTLE to enter a card battle with an AI opponent. You will use your own deck, which you can edit later.

Each player draws 5 cards at the start of the game. Click on an orange BATTLER card to place it. The AI will do the same blindly. These two cards are going to battle! 

BATTLER cards have an ATK stat and an HP stat. Below is an example.

_________
| Jeremy |
|        |
|        |
|A4  H5/5|
|________|

Jeremy has an ATK stat of 4, and an HP stat of 5. Jeremy still has 5 out of 5 HP remaining.

When the two cards battle, their attack is subtracted from the other's HP. If only one card survives the fight, that card's player earns a point. Earn 3 points to win!

There are also BOOST cards. When each player's BATTLER is revealed, both players can play a BOOST card to turn the tides of the fight before the cards battle. BOOST cards have various effects, so check them all out! Your BOOST cards resolve before the opponent's.

If you win a BATTLE against the AI, you get credits! Use those credits to buy cards from the SHOP.


/// SHOP ///

In the SHOP, you can use your credits you've earned from BATTLEs to buy new cards! Each card costs 50 credits. You can exit and re-enter the shop to refresh it.

/// DECK EDIT ///

In DECK EDIT, you can customize your deck with the cards you've earned. Click DECK to view the cards in your deck. Click COLLECTION to view the other cards you've collected! Click on cards to take them in or out of your deck.

Build a powerful deck that the opponent can't defeat, or collect every card in the game. The world of card games is your oyster! Hope you like seafood, BATTLER!
