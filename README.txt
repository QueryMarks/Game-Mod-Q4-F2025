This file explains the CARD BATTLER mod for Quake 4. To play this mod, place the coolmod folder in your Quake 4 directory. Select it from the mods list after starting the game.

Most of this game is contained within the pause menu in the game's singleplayer mode. Start a new game to get started.

Once you're in a new game, press ESC to view the pause menu. There are three new options: BATTLE, SHOP, and DECK EDIT.

/// BATTLE ///

This is the main event! Click BATTLE to enter a card battle with an AI opponent. You will use your own deck, which you can edit later.

Each player draws 5 cards at the start of the game. Click on an orange BATTLER card to place it face down. The AI will do the same, then both are flipped face up. These two cards are going to battle! 

BATTLER cards have an ATK stat and an HP stat. Below is an example.

_________
| Jeremy |
|        |
|        |
|A4  H5/5|
|________|

Jeremy has an ATK stat of 4, and an HP stat of 5. Jeremy still has 5 out of 5 HP remaining.

Some powerful BOSS cards can't be played on their own. If you have a leftover card that survived a battle, you can play a BOSS card by replacing your weaker card that's already battled! You can get rid of weakened cards this way AND get a much stronger card out to battle in the process.

When the two cards battle, their attack is subtracted from the other's HP. If only one card survives the fight, that card's player earns a point. Earn 3 points to win!

There are also BOOST cards. When each player's BATTLER is revealed, both players can play a BOOST card to turn the tides of the fight before the cards battle. BOOST cards have various effects, so check them all out!

If you win a BATTLE against the AI, you get credits! Use those credits to buy cards from the SHOP.

/// SHOP ///

In the SHOP, you can use your credits you've earned from BATTLEs to buy new cards! Card packs are bundled by the type of cards they have, and you get 3 per pack.

/// DECK EDIT ///

In DECK EDIT, you can customize your deck with the cards you've earned. On the left, your current deck is displayed. On the right, your collection is displayed. Scroll through your collection with the arrows at the bottom, and click on the cards to add them to your deck. Click on cards in your deck to remove them. When you're done, hit SAVE to save the changes and hit EXIT to get back to the pause menu. Make sure you save your changes! You must have a deck of 20-30 cards to SAVE.

Build a powerful deck that the opponent can't defeat, or collect every card in the game. The world of card games is your oyster! Hope you like seafood, BATTLER!
